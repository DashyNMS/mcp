DashyNMS MCP 2.0 serves tools over HTTP as well as stdio, gives its operator control over what clients can do, and follows LibreNMS 26.10 and 26.11.

## Breaking

- **Device states are reported as LibreNMS reports them:** ignored, maintenance and unreported devices have their own words in output, filters and counts, so a filter for `up` or `down` no longer takes them in (#121).

## New

- **HTTP:** tools over authenticated Streamable HTTP, with a named token for each client, health and readiness checks, and limits so one token can't use every connection (#17).
- **Run it without Docker:** one self-contained program for Windows, macOS and Linux, on x64 and ARM64, with checksums and build provenance (#36).
- **Tool policy:** allow and deny lists that apply to what's offered and what can be called (#37).
- **Limits:** a cap on requests in flight to LibreNMS, Unimus and Graylog, and on calls a client makes in a minute (#38).
- **Audit:** a redacted entry for every call, on stderr or sent to Graylog over GELF (#18).
- **Structured answers:** every tool declares its output schema (#40).
- **Last known good:** when LibreNMS is down, the last answer is given and marked stale with its time (#39).
- **Help:** `dashynms_help` answers how-to questions from the Desktop, Mobile and MCP user guides, marking the product and release. It needs no LibreNMS connection (#164).
- **User guide:** installing, connecting an assistant, sharing a server, settings and troubleshooting, on the [wiki](https://github.com/DashyNMS/mcp/wiki) (#163).
- **Prompts and resources:** built-in investigations, and read-only resources such as a network overview (#20).
- **Maintenance** shows on device search rows, and sensor readings are judged by your own thresholds and installed state definitions (#94).
- **Unimus:** devices match by short hostname with a configured discovery domain (#157).
- **For app hosts:** the read-only operations are published as `DashyNMS.Agent.Shared`, which DashyNMS Mobile runs in process.

## Changed

- **Versions:** the image, the programs and `DashyNMS.Agent.Shared` are published together as one version. `edge` follows every change to `main` and `nightly` each night's (#172).
- **Alerts** are grouped by device and rule as LibreNMS 26.10 does, and `alert_rules` shows settings for each entity and the columns 26.11 removed.

## Fixed

- **Large networks:** sensor questions are answered without global read, saying how many devices were covered (#71), and event windows reach further back on busy networks, naming any gap (#70).
- **LibreNMS 26.11:** VLAN numbers and rule warnings (#156).
- **Unknown stays unknown:** missing readings, refused permissions, failed history and incomplete log searches are said to be so rather than answered as empty or healthy.
- **Alerts:** history and recoveries match by fault, and counts agree across every tool.
- **Redaction:** secrets in config diffs with awkward XML, tags or line breaks stay hidden.
